**Learning objective:** By the end of this lesson, students will be able to use GenAI to analyze customer feedback and assess whether a product demonstrates market fit. Identify gaps, unmet needs, and potential improvements based on real-world input.


## Scenario: Is this product a market fit?

You're working with feedback from a **Smart Grocery Cart** pilot. This tech-enabled cart helps in-store shoppers scan items, track budgets, and get real-time recipe suggestions.

The dataset includes feedback from **shoppers, store managers, and employees**. Some love it. Others are overwhelmed or unsure how to use it.

---

## Dataset

Use this file:

- [smart_grocery_cart_feedback.csv](assets/smart_grocery_cart_feedback.csv)

It includes:

- `comment_text`
- `store_location`
- `shopping_frequency`
- `tech_comfort_level`
- `user_role`
- `timestamp`
- `purchase_amount`

---

## You have 25 minutes. Here's what to do:

- **Start with the core question.**  
  *Is this product a good market fit?*  
  What other questions could help you answer this?

- **Use your skills.**  
  Segment the data. Create a focused GenAI prompt. Analyze the results.  
  Use your understanding of strong analysis and smart prompting.

- **Prepare actionable insights.**  
  - If the product shows signs of fit, what should the company do next?  
  - If it doesn't, what needs to change?  
  - What would you recommend to a product or strategy team?

---

## Optional: Share your thinking

If working in pairs or small groups, discuss:

- What signals of product/market fit—or lack of it—stood out?  
- What did GenAI surface that surprised you?  
- What’s one action you’d recommend based on what you found?

> 💡 Share one insight and one next step you’d suggest for the team.
