## Thinking in Steps: How to Decompose Tasks for AI

Modern marketing analytics mixes structured data (like engagement rates) and unstructured data (like customer feedback). 

> 💡 **Imagine this:** After a campaign, you receive a spreadsheet of metrics and a giant pile of customer comments. Parsing the numbers is easy, but summarizing the free-text feedback could take *hours*.

AI tools can help us here, but only if we break up the work into well-scoped tasks. You wouldn't ask a sous-chef to "make dinner" without a recipe. Likewise, you can't expect great results from AI without clear direction.

## What makes a task AI-compatible?

> 💡 **Think of AI like a kitchen assistant.** It can chop, stir, and prep with speed—but it needs clear instructions. You still decide the flavor, timing, and presentation.

**AI-compatible sub-tasks** usually share these traits:

* Clear instructions and expectations
* Objective outputs or repeatable patterns
* Language-heavy work that’s time-consuming for humans

**Examples:**

* Summarizing survey feedback
* Identifying sentiment (positive/neutral/negative)
* Grouping comments into themes
* Drafting first-pass summaries for reports
* Brainstorming headlines or taglines under constraints

**Leave to humans:**

* Adding business context
* Fact-checking and quality control
* Making final recommendations

> 🏆 Use AI for repeatable language work. Save your energy for interpreting results, making decisions, and adding insight.

## Walkthrough: Email campaign analysis

Let’s break down a real scenario. You’re asked to analyze a recent email launch.

**You have:**

* Performance data (opens, clicks, conversions)
* Free-text feedback from customers
* Internal notes from a debrief meeting

**Don’t do this:** Paste it all into ChatGPT and hope for magic.

**Instead, break it down.**

### Sub-task 1: Summarize customer feedback

> **Prompt:**
> "Summarize the main themes and sentiment from the following customer responses. List three key takeaways and provide a one-sentence summary of the overall mood."

### Sub-task 2: Extract action items from internal notes

> **Prompt:**
> "Review these internal campaign notes and extract the main action items and any open questions in a bullet list."

## Before you try it: A few reminders

* **Context:** AI doesn't know your brand voice, strategy, or audience unless you tell it.
* **Privacy:** Never paste in personally identifiable info or sensitive data.
* **Accuracy:** Always review outputs like you would an intern's draft.
